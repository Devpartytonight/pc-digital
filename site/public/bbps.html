What it is

How BBPS works for your business

The Bharat Bill Payment System is an integrated, interoperable bill-payment ecosystem conceptualised by the Reserve Bank of India and operated under NPCI Bharat BillPay Limited. It lets customers pay many different billers through one standard, trusted channel.

With PC Digital, you access BBPS billers through authorised partner operating units. You fetch the bill using the customer’s consumer number, show them the amount due, collect the payment and issue a BBPS reference — one flow for hundreds of billers.

Service availability, limits, charges and commissions are subject to KYC, the rules of the relevant service provider and applicable regulations. See our Disclaimer.

Questions

BBPS FAQs

Can’t find your answer? Ask our support team.

Is BBPS safe for my customers?
BBPS is a regulated bill-payment system with standardised rules for confirmation, settlement and complaints. Each payment carries a unique reference number that can be verified with the biller.
How soon is the bill marked as paid?
Confirmation is instant for most billers. Some billers update their own records after a delay defined by the biller; the BBPS reference remains valid proof of payment during that time.
Can a bill payment be reversed?
A successful payment made to the biller cannot be cancelled by PC Digital. If the amount was debited but the payment failed, it is reversed as per our Refund & Cancellation Policy.
Which billers are available?
Biller availability is defined by the BBPS network and our partner operating units, and is shown live in your panel.
