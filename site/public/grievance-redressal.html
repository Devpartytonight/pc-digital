Grievance Redressal

How to raise a complaint, what to expect and how to escalate it.

At PC Digital Private Limited ("PC Digital", "we", "us"), we want every Partner and Customer to have a clear, fair and timely way to raise a concern. This page explains how to register a complaint, what to expect, and how to escalate if you are not satisfied.

01What You Can Raise

  • A transaction that failed, is pending, or was debited but not completed.
  • A refund or reversal that has not been received.
  • Wallet load, settlement or commission queries.
  • Issues with onboarding, KYC or account access.
  • A complaint about the conduct of a PC Digital Partner outlet, including overcharging.
  • Suspected fraud or unauthorised use of your account.
  • Concerns about how your personal data has been handled.

02Before You Write to Us

Keeping these details ready helps us resolve your complaint faster:

  • your registered mobile number or Partner ID;
  • the transaction ID or reference number (for example RRN, UTR, BBPS reference or PNR);
  • the date, amount and service involved; and
  • a short description of the problem, with a screenshot or receipt if available.

03Level 1 – Customer Support

Contact our support team by phone or email. Most queries are resolved at this level.

  • Phone: 8169108855 / 8169508855
  • Email: Support@pcdigital.in

You will receive an acknowledgement with a reference for your complaint. We aim to acknowledge complaints within 24 hours and to resolve them within 7 working days. Where a complaint depends on a bank, operator, biller or travel supplier, resolution may take longer and we will keep you informed.

04Level 2 – Grievance Officer

If your complaint is not resolved at Level 1 within the time indicated, or you are not satisfied with the resolution, you may escalate it to our Grievance Officer. Please mention your earlier complaint reference.

The Grievance Officer will acknowledge the escalation within 48 hours and endeavour to resolve it within 15 days of receipt, and in any case within the timelines prescribed under applicable law.

05Level 3 – Service Provider and External Escalation

Some services are governed by the complaint frameworks of the banks and networks that provide them. If your complaint remains unresolved after Level 2, you may also:

  • AEPS, DMT and UPI: raise a complaint with your own bank using the transaction reference. If the bank does not resolve it within 30 days, you may approach the Reserve Bank of India under the Reserve Bank – Integrated Ombudsman Scheme through the RBI's Complaint Management System.
  • BBPS bill payments: register or track a complaint under the Bharat Bill Payment System's complaint-management process using the BBPS transaction reference.
  • Rail tickets: refund and TDR matters are decided by IRCTC and Indian Railways under their rules.
  • Flights and hotels: matters relating to the service itself are decided by the airline or hotel concerned.

We will share transaction details with you to support any such escalation.

06Reporting Fraud

If you suspect fraud or unauthorised activity, contact us immediately on 8169108855 / 8169508855 so that we can secure your account. Financial cyber-fraud may also be reported to the National Cyber Crime Helpline on 1930 or on the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal. PC Digital will never ask for your password, MPIN or OTP.

07Our Commitment

  • Every complaint is recorded and tracked to closure.
  • Complaints are handled fairly, without discrimination, and in confidence.
  • We do not charge any fee for registering or resolving a complaint.
  • We review complaints regularly to improve our services and processes.
