All FAQs
What is PC Digital?
PC Digital Private Limited is a fintech company based in Dombivali (Thane), Maharashtra. We provide a single platform through which retailers, distributors and businesses can offer or use recharge, BBPS bill payment, AEPS, money transfer, payout, UPI and travel-booking services.
Who can become a PC Digital partner?
Any Indian resident aged 18 or above with a shop or place of business, a valid PAN, Aadhaar and a bank account in their own name. Kirana stores, mobile shops, medical stores, travel agents, cyber cafés and similar outlets are all a good fit. Distributors and businesses can also register.
Which services can I offer?
Recharge & DTH, BBPS bill payments, AEPS, Domestic Money Transfer, Payout services, UPI collection & payouts, hotel booking, flight booking and IRCTC rail booking. Some services need additional approval or a device, such as a biometric scanner for AEPS.
How do I earn?
You earn a commission or margin on successful transactions. The applicable structure for each service is shown in your partner panel. Actual earnings depend on your location, footfall and the services you offer; we do not promise a fixed income.
Is PC Digital a bank?
No. PC Digital is a technology platform. Banking and payment services such as AEPS, DMT and UPI are provided in association with partner banks and authorised service providers.
