About PC Digital

Digital finance, closer to home.

We are PC Digital Private Limited — a fintech company from Dombivali, Thane, on a simple mission: to make everyday financial and digital services available at the shop around the corner.

Our story

Why we built PC Digital.

India pays digitally now. But for a very large number of people, the last step is still a human one: a shopkeeper they know, who can recharge a phone, pay an electricity bill, withdraw cash from an Aadhaar-linked account or send money to a family member in another state.

That shopkeeper is the real front line of digital finance — and too often they are left managing five different apps, five different balances and nobody to call when a transaction gets stuck.

PC Digital was set up to fix that. We bring recharge and DTH, BBPS bill payments, AEPS, Domestic Money Transfer, payouts, UPI collection and travel bookings onto one platform, behind one partner ID and one wallet, with a support team that answers the phone.

Our tagline says what we believe: Digital Finance • Smarter Future. When a neighbourhood counter can do more, the retailer earns more, the customer travels less, and the whole locality gets a little closer to the formal financial system.

Our mission

Make financial and digital services reachable for everyone, through the shops they already trust.

We do it by giving retailers simple tools, honest processes and dependable support, so that offering a banking or payment service is as easy as selling any other product on the shelf.

Our vision

A smarter financial future where no customer is more than a short walk from digital finance.

We want every PC Digital outlet to be a point of confidence in its locality: a place where a transaction is quick, the receipt is real and the person across the counter is accountable.

What we stand for

Five things we don’t compromise on.

These are the standards we hold ourselves to, and the ones we ask of every partner who carries our name on their counter.

01Trust firstA financial service is only as good as the confidence people have in it. We verify every partner and stand behind every transaction record.
