KYC process for onboarding

Verified in, trusted out.

Every PC Digital partner is verified before activation. Here is exactly what the KYC process involves, which documents you need and the conditions that apply.

PANAadhaarBank proofBusiness proofLive photo
The process

Six steps, start to activation.

Most of it happens on your phone. Keep your original documents and your Aadhaar-linked mobile number handy.

01RegisterShare your name, mobile number, email and the type of partnership you want. Verify your mobile number with an OTP.
02Verify PANEnter your PAN. The name and status are verified against official records. PAN is mandatory for every partner.
03Verify AadhaarComplete Aadhaar-based e-KYC with your consent, using an OTP sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile number, or submit another officially valid document.
04Add bank accountProvide your account number and IFSC with a cancelled cheque or passbook copy. The account must be in your own or your business’s name and is verified before use.
05Business & outlet detailsShare your shop or business name, address and proof, a live photograph of yourself and photographs of the outlet.
06Review & activationOur team reviews your application. Once approved, your partner ID is activated and services are enabled. Some services need additional approval from the service provider.

Conditions

Conditions of onboarding

  • 18 years or olderThe applicant must be an adult Indian resident, competent to contract.
  • Everything in your own namePAN, Aadhaar, bank account and application must belong to the same person or entity.
  • One ID per personA partner ID is personal and non-transferable. It cannot be operated by anyone else.
  • A real place of businessA fixed shop or office address that can be verified.
  • Wallet funded from your own accountTop-ups must come from your registered bank account. Third-party and cash deposits may be rejected.
  • Re-KYC when askedKYC is refreshed periodically and whenever your details change.
  • Approval is not automaticPC Digital and its service providers may decline or limit an application based on verification and risk review.
  • Fees are non-refundableAny onboarding or agent-ID fee is non-refundable once verification begins.
Your responsibilities

Once you are live

  • Verify every customerCollect only what the service needs and transact only in the customer’s presence, on their instruction.
  • Never store Aadhaar or biometricsCustomer Aadhaar numbers, fingerprints and OTPs must never be saved, copied or shared.
  • Charge only what is permittedDo not collect anything above the prescribed customer charges. Always give a receipt.
  • No split or fictitious transactionsDo not break up transactions to avoid limits or to earn extra commission.
  • Keep your login to yourselfNever share your password, MPIN or OTP — not even with someone claiming to be from PC Digital.
  • Report anything suspiciousTell us immediately if a transaction or customer seems doubtful.

KYC questions

Before you start.

How long does KYC take?
If your documents are clear and your details match, verification is usually quick. Timelines can be longer for services that need approval from a bank or another service provider, such as AEPS or rail ticketing.
My Aadhaar is not linked to my mobile number. Can I still apply?
Aadhaar OTP e-KYC needs a linked mobile number. You can update your mobile number at an Aadhaar centre, or speak to our team about the alternative documents we can accept.
Can I use a family member’s bank account?
No. The bank account must be in the name of the applicant or the applicant’s business. This is a regulatory requirement.
Why was my application rejected?
Common reasons are name mismatches between PAN, Aadhaar and bank account, unclear document images, or an unverifiable address. You can re-apply with corrected documents.
Is my data safe?
Your documents are used only for verification and regulatory purposes, as described in our Privacy Policy. We do not store biometric data.
Is there a fee to join?
Any applicable onboarding or service-specific registration fee will be explained to you by our team before you pay. Pay only into the official PC Digital company account.
