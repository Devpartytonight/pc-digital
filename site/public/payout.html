What it is

How Payout Services works for your business

Growing businesses make dozens of outgoing payments every day: supplier invoices, commissions, refunds, salaries and incentives. Doing them one at a time through net banking is slow and hard to reconcile.

With PC Digital Payout, you load funds once and pay many beneficiaries from a single dashboard. Each payout carries a bank reference (UTR) and a live status, so your accounts team always knows what has been paid and what is pending. Payouts are processed through partner banks and authorised payment partners.

Service availability, limits, charges and commissions are subject to KYC, the rules of the relevant service provider and applicable regulations. See our Disclaimer.

Questions

Payout Services FAQs

Can’t find your answer? Ask our support team.

Who can use Payout Services?
Registered businesses and verified PC Digital partners with completed business KYC. Payouts must be for legitimate business purposes that you declare during onboarding.
How fast are payouts?
IMPS and UPI payouts are processed in near real time. NEFT and RTGS follow banking system timings.
What happens to a failed payout?
The amount is reversed to your payout balance once the bank confirms the failure or return.
Are there restricted uses?
Yes. Payouts may not be used for any unlawful activity, gaming or betting prohibited by law, or any activity restricted by our partner banks. See our Terms & Conditions.
