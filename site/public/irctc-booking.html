What it is

How IRCTC Booking works for your business

Rail remains the way most of India travels, and many passengers prefer to book through a nearby shop they trust. With rail booking on PC Digital, you can search trains, check seat availability across classes and book e-tickets for your customers.

Rail e-ticketing is offered through authorised IRCTC service-provider arrangements. Each booking outlet requires an agent ID registered in the partner’s own name, and bookings must follow IRCTC’s rules for authorised agents, including booking-window restrictions.

Service availability, limits, charges and commissions are subject to KYC, the rules of the relevant service provider and applicable regulations. See our Disclaimer.

Questions

IRCTC Booking FAQs

Can’t find your answer? Ask our support team.

Do I need a separate ID for rail booking?
Yes. Rail e-ticketing requires an agent ID registered in your own name. Our onboarding team will guide you through the application and applicable registration charges.
How are refunds for cancelled tickets processed?
Refunds are calculated under Indian Railways’ refund rules and are credited after IRCTC processes the cancellation. Convenience and service fees are generally non-refundable.
Are there restrictions on agents?
Yes. Authorised agents must follow IRCTC rules, including restrictions on booking during the opening period of advance-reservation and Tatkal windows, and must not overcharge customers.
Who is responsible for train delays or cancellations?
Indian Railways. PC Digital facilitates the booking only. See our Disclaimer.
