What it is

How UPI Collection & Payouts works for your business

The Unified Payments Interface (UPI), built by NPCI, is India’s most widely used real-time payment system. Customers expect to scan and pay — and businesses expect to know instantly whether that payment arrived.

PC Digital gives merchants and businesses UPI collection tools and UPI payouts through partner banks and authorised payment partners. Each collection is matched to its order or customer reference, so you can stop checking phone screenshots as proof of payment.

Service availability, limits, charges and commissions are subject to KYC, the rules of the relevant service provider and applicable regulations. See our Disclaimer.

Questions

UPI Collection & Payouts FAQs

Can’t find your answer? Ask our support team.

Which UPI apps can my customers use?
Any UPI-enabled app. UPI is interoperable, so the QR works across apps and banks.
When do I receive the money collected?
Collections are settled to your registered bank account or balance as per the settlement cycle communicated at onboarding.
A customer paid but I don’t see it. What now?
Ask the customer for the UPI transaction reference (UTR) and raise it with our support team. UPI transactions that fail after debit are reversed to the payer by their bank under NPCI rules.
Can I use this for personal transfers?
No. The service is for genuine merchant and business payments only.
