What it is

How Recharge / DTH works for your business

Mobile and DTH recharges are the most frequent digital transaction at a neighbourhood shop. PC Digital brings the major telecom and DTH operators together on one screen, so you never have to juggle separate operator balances or multiple apps.

Enter the number, pick the operator and plan, confirm, and the recharge is processed in real time. Every transaction is recorded in your ledger with its operator reference so you can answer a customer query on the spot.

Service availability, limits, charges and commissions are subject to KYC, the rules of the relevant service provider and applicable regulations. See our Disclaimer.

Questions

Recharge / DTH FAQs

Can’t find your answer? Ask our support team.

Which operators are supported?
Major prepaid mobile and DTH operators in India are supported. The live operator list is shown in your partner panel, as availability can change when operators revise their integrations.
What happens if a recharge fails?
If the operator rejects the transaction, the amount is reversed to your wallet. If a transaction stays in “pending”, it is reconciled with the operator and either confirmed or reversed. See our Refund & Cancellation Policy for details.
Can a successful recharge be cancelled?
No. Once an operator confirms a recharge it cannot be cancelled or reversed, including recharges made to a wrong number. Please verify the number with the customer before confirming.
How do I earn?
You earn a commission on every successful recharge. The applicable commission structure is shown in your partner panel and may vary by operator.
