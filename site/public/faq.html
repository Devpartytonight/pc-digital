General

What is PC Digital?
PC Digital Private Limited is a fintech company based in Dombivali (Thane), Maharashtra. We provide a single platform through which retailers, distributors and businesses can offer or use recharge, BBPS bill payment, AEPS, money transfer, payout, UPI and travel-booking services.
Who can become a PC Digital partner?
Any Indian resident aged 18 or above with a shop or place of business, a valid PAN, Aadhaar and a bank account in their own name. Kirana stores, mobile shops, medical stores, travel agents, cyber cafés and similar outlets are all a good fit. Distributors and businesses can also register.
Which services can I offer?
Recharge & DTH, BBPS bill payments, AEPS, Domestic Money Transfer, Payout services, UPI collection & payouts, hotel booking, flight booking and IRCTC rail booking. Some services need additional approval or a device, such as a biometric scanner for AEPS.
How do I earn?
You earn a commission or margin on successful transactions. The applicable structure for each service is shown in your partner panel. Actual earnings depend on your location, footfall and the services you offer; we do not promise a fixed income.
Is PC Digital a bank?
No. PC Digital is a technology platform. Banking and payment services such as AEPS, DMT and UPI are provided in association with partner banks and authorised service providers.
02 / 05

Joining & KYC

How do I become a partner?
Fill in the form on our Become a Partner page or call our support numbers. Our team will guide you through KYC and activation.
Which documents do I need?
PAN, Aadhaar (or another officially valid document), a bank account proof in your own name, proof of your shop or business, and a live photograph. The full list is on our KYC & Onboarding page.
Do I need a computer?
No. A smartphone with internet is enough for most services. AEPS additionally needs a registered fingerprint device.
Can I be both a retailer and a distributor?
Roles have separate IDs and KYC. Speak to our team about the right structure for your business.
Can I transfer my ID to someone else?
No. A partner ID is issued after verifying a specific person or entity and is non-transferable.
03 / 05

Wallet & settlement

How do I add money to my wallet?
Transfer funds from your own registered bank account using the methods shown in your panel and raise a load request with the payment reference. Balance is credited once the funds are confirmed.
Can someone else deposit money for me?
No. Wallet top-ups must come from your own registered bank account. Third-party or cash deposits may be rejected and returned.
When do I get my AEPS settlement?
AEPS withdrawal amounts are credited to your wallet as per the settlement cycle communicated at onboarding, which depends on partner bank timelines.
How is commission paid?
Commission on successful transactions is credited as per the structure shown in your panel, after deduction of TDS as required by law.
Can I withdraw my wallet balance?
Wallet balance is meant for transactions on the platform. On closing your account, the remaining balance is refunded to your registered bank account after verification and settlement of dues.
04 / 05

Transactions & refunds

A transaction shows “pending”. What should I do?
Do not repeat it immediately. Pending transactions are reconciled with the service provider and will be marked success or reversed. If it stays pending, contact support with the transaction ID.
A transaction failed but money was deducted.
Failed transactions are reversed to your wallet, or to the customer’s bank account in the case of AEPS and UPI, under the timelines in our Refund & Cancellation Policy.
I entered a wrong number or account. Can it be reversed?
A successful transaction processed on the details entered cannot be reversed by PC Digital. Please verify details with the customer before confirming.
How do I raise a complaint?
Call or email support with your transaction reference. If you are not satisfied, escalate as described on our Grievance Redressal page.
05 / 05

Safety & security

Will PC Digital ever ask for my OTP or password?
Never. No one from PC Digital will ask for your password, MPIN or OTP. Do not share them with anyone.
How do I know a call or message is really from PC Digital?
Our official contact details are the phone numbers and email on this website. If in doubt, disconnect and call us back on those numbers.
Do you store customers’ fingerprints?
No. Biometric data for AEPS is captured through a registered device and sent in encrypted form for authentication. PC Digital does not store it.
What should I do if I suspect fraud?
Contact us immediately so we can secure your account. You can also report financial cyber-fraud to the National Cyber Crime Helpline on 1930.
