What it is

How Flight Booking works for your business

First-time flyers and busy customers alike value a familiar face who can find a good fare and handle the booking. PC Digital gives you airline inventory through our travel supply partners, with live fares and availability.

You see the fare break-up, baggage allowance and the airline’s change and cancellation rules up front, so there are no surprises for you or your customer later.

Questions

Flight Booking FAQs

Can’t find your answer? Ask our support team.

How are cancellations and refunds handled?
Refunds depend on the airline’s fare rules for that ticket. Airline cancellation charges and applicable service fees are deducted, and the balance is credited after the airline processes the refund.
Can a name be corrected after booking?
Name changes are governed by each airline and are often not permitted. Please verify passenger names against their ID before issuing the ticket.
Who is responsible for delays or cancellations by the airline?
The airline. PC Digital facilitates the booking and will help you raise refund requests where the airline permits. See our Disclaimer.
