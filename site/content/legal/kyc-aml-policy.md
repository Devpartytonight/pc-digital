**PC Digital Private Limited** ("PC Digital", "we", "us") is committed to preventing the use of its platform for money laundering, terrorist financing, fraud or any other unlawful activity. This KYC & AML Policy sets out the principles we follow when onboarding and monitoring Partners, merchants and businesses, and the conditions that apply to them. It should be read with our [Terms & Conditions](terms-and-conditions.html) and the step-by-step guide on our [KYC & Onboarding](kyc-onboarding.html) page.

This Policy is guided by the Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002 and the rules made under it, the Reserve Bank of India's directions on Know Your Customer as applicable to our partner banks and to the services they provide through us, and the requirements of NPCI, UIDAI and our other Service Providers.

## Objectives

- To know and verify the identity of every Partner, merchant and business we onboard.
- To understand the nature of their business and the purpose for which they use our services.
- To monitor transactions for unusual or suspicious activity.
- To maintain records and cooperate with partner banks, regulators and law-enforcement agencies as required by law.

## Who Must Complete KYC

KYC is mandatory before activation for:

- retailers and agents;
- distributors and master distributors;
- merchants using UPI collection;
- businesses using payout services, including their authorised signatories and beneficial owners; and
- applicants for a rail ticketing agent ID.

No service is activated on the basis of incomplete KYC.

## Documents Required

### Individuals and proprietors

- PAN card (mandatory).
- Aadhaar, verified through means permitted by law, or another officially valid document such as a passport, voter ID or driving licence.
- Recent photograph or live selfie.
- Proof of bank account in the applicant's name: cancelled cheque, passbook or bank statement.
- Proof of business and outlet address, such as a Shop & Establishment certificate, Udyam registration, GST certificate, utility bill or rent agreement.
- Photographs of the outlet, where applicable.

### Partnership firms, LLPs and companies

- PAN of the entity.
- Certificate of registration or incorporation, and the partnership deed, LLP agreement or Memorandum and Articles of Association.
- GST registration certificate, where applicable.
- Board resolution or authorisation letter naming the authorised signatory.
- KYC documents of partners, directors, authorised signatories and beneficial owners.
- Proof of the entity's bank account and registered address.

Additional documents may be requested depending on the service, risk assessment or the requirements of a partner bank.

## Verification

- Documents and details are verified against issuing-authority databases wherever available, including PAN verification and bank account (penny-drop) verification.
- Aadhaar-based e-KYC is carried out only with the applicant's consent, using OTP or biometric authentication through authorised channels.
- We may conduct video verification, tele-verification or a physical visit to the outlet.
- The name on the PAN, the bank account and the application must match. Accounts cannot be opened in one person's name and operated by another.
- Applicants are screened against sanctions and other adverse lists as required.

## Conditions of Onboarding

- The applicant must be at least 18 years old and an Indian resident with a fixed place of business.
- One individual may hold only one Partner ID of the same type, unless specifically approved.
- Applications with forged, altered, mismatched or unclear documents will be rejected, and may be reported to the appropriate authorities.
- PC Digital may decline any application, or restrict the services offered to a Partner, based on its risk assessment or the decision of a partner bank or Service Provider. Reasons may not always be disclosed where the law so requires.
- Onboarding fees, where charged, are non-refundable once verification has begun, as described in our [Refund & Cancellation Policy](refund-cancellation-policy.html).

## Risk Categorisation and Ongoing Due Diligence

- Partners are categorised by risk, based on factors such as location, nature of business, services used and transaction patterns.
- KYC information is updated periodically (re-KYC), and whenever there is a material change in the Partner's details, or when we or a partner bank consider it necessary.
- AEPS agents are required to complete periodic authentication as mandated by NPCI and the partner bank.

## Customer Due Diligence by Partners

Partners are the first point of contact with Customers and must:

- identify each Customer and collect only the information and documents required for the service requested;
- register money-transfer senders using their own mobile number and the KYC prescribed for remitters, and respect the applicable transaction limits;
- obtain the Customer's explicit consent and live biometric or OTP authentication for Aadhaar-based transactions, and never store Aadhaar numbers or biometrics;
- never split transactions or use multiple sender profiles to get around limits;
- refuse any transaction where the Customer's identity or the purpose of the transaction is doubtful; and
- report suspicious behaviour to PC Digital immediately at Support@pcdigital.in.

## Transaction Monitoring

We monitor transactions on the Platform for patterns that may indicate misuse, such as unusually high volumes, repeated transactions of similar value, many transactions to the same beneficiary from different senders, transactions inconsistent with the Partner's profile, or Wallet funding from third-party accounts. Where such patterns are found, we may seek an explanation and documents, place limits, hold settlements, or suspend the account pending review.

## Wallet Funding

- Wallets must be funded only from the Partner's own registered bank account, through the modes we permit.
- Cash deposits and third-party deposits may be rejected or held for verification, and returned to the source.

## Reporting and Cooperation

- Suspicious transactions are escalated to the concerned partner bank or Service Provider and reported as required by law.
- We cooperate fully with lawful requests from regulators, courts and law-enforcement agencies, and may share Partner and transaction information with them without notice to the Partner where the law requires.
- Partners must not disclose ("tip off") to any Customer or third party that a transaction has been reported or is under review.

## Record Retention

KYC records and transaction records are retained for the periods prescribed under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002 and other Applicable Law, which is generally at least five years from the date of the transaction or from the end of the business relationship, as applicable.

## Consequences of Non-Compliance

Breach of this Policy may lead to one or more of the following: suspension or termination of the Partner ID, withholding of Wallet balances and settlements pending investigation, recovery of losses and penalties, and reporting to partner banks, regulators and law-enforcement agencies.

## Training and Review

We provide guidance to our team and Partners on KYC and AML requirements, and review this Policy periodically to keep it aligned with changes in law and Service Provider requirements.
