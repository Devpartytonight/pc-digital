This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains how **PC Digital Private Limited** ("PC Digital", "we", "us") handles failed transactions, reversals, cancellations and refunds for services offered on www.pcdigital.in, our portal and mobile application (the "Platform"). It forms part of our [Terms & Conditions](terms-and-conditions.html).

Most services on the Platform are delivered in real time by third-party Service Providers such as banks, operators, billers, airlines, hotels and IRCTC. For that reason, a transaction that has been successfully completed generally **cannot be cancelled or refunded by PC Digital**. Refunds apply mainly where a transaction has failed, or where the Service Provider's own rules allow a cancellation.

## General Principles

- Please check all details — mobile number, consumer number, Aadhaar number, bank account and IFSC, UPI ID, passenger and guest names, dates and amounts — before confirming a transaction. Transactions processed using details entered by the Partner or Customer are treated as correctly executed.
- Refunds are made only to the source from which the payment was received: the Partner's Wallet, the payout balance, or the original bank account or payment instrument. Refunds are not paid in cash by PC Digital.
- Where a refund is due to an end Customer, the Partner who collected the money is responsible for passing it on to the Customer once the amount is credited back.
- Any commission or incentive paid on a transaction that is later refunded or reversed will be recovered.
- Timelines stated in this Policy are indicative. Actual timelines depend on the Service Provider, the banking system and bank holidays.

## Mobile and DTH Recharge

- A successful recharge cannot be cancelled, reversed or transferred to another number, including recharges made to a wrong number or for a wrong amount or plan.
- If a recharge fails, the amount is reversed to the Partner's Wallet, usually immediately.
- If a recharge remains "pending", it is reconciled with the operator. It will be either confirmed as successful or reversed to the Wallet, typically within 3 to 5 working days.
- If a recharge is shown as successful but the benefit has not been received, raise a complaint with the transaction reference within 48 hours. We will take it up with the operator, whose decision is final.

## BBPS Bill Payments

- A bill payment that has been successfully processed to the biller cannot be cancelled or refunded by PC Digital. Excess or duplicate payments must be taken up by the Customer with the biller, and are usually adjusted by the biller against future bills.
- If the amount is debited but the payment fails, it is reversed to the Wallet, typically within 3 to 5 working days.
- Complaints about bill payments may be raised with the BBPS transaction reference and will be handled under the BBPS complaint-management framework.

## AEPS

- AEPS transactions are processed between the Customer's bank and the acquiring bank. If a cash-withdrawal transaction fails but the Customer's bank account is debited, the amount is returned to the Customer's account by the Customer's bank through the standard reversal process prescribed by NPCI and the RBI, generally within 5 working days.
- The Partner must not hand over cash for a transaction that is not shown as successful, and must not ask the Customer to repeat a transaction without checking its status.
- A Customer may also raise a complaint directly with their own bank using the transaction reference (RRN).
- Balance enquiry and mini-statement transactions are non-financial and involve no refund.

## Domestic Money Transfer (DMT)

- A transfer that has been successfully credited to the beneficiary account number provided cannot be cancelled or recalled by PC Digital, including where the account number was entered incorrectly. In such cases, the sender must approach the beneficiary bank; we will provide transaction details to assist.
- If a transfer fails or is returned by the beneficiary bank, the amount becomes eligible for refund. Refund to the sender is initiated through the Partner and may require OTP confirmation from the sender's registered mobile number.
- Pending transfers are reconciled with the partner bank and are either completed or reversed, typically within 3 to 7 working days.
- Customer charges collected on a successful transfer are non-refundable.

## Payout Services

- A payout that has been successfully credited to the beneficiary cannot be reversed by PC Digital.
- Failed or returned payouts are credited back to the payout balance after confirmation from the bank, usually within 1 to 5 working days.
- Fees charged on successful payouts are non-refundable.

## UPI Collection and UPI Payouts

- UPI transactions are final once successfully completed. A payer who has paid a merchant in error must resolve the matter with the merchant.
- If a UPI transaction fails after the payer's account is debited, the amount is reversed to the payer by their bank in accordance with NPCI's rules, generally within 5 working days.
- Disputes and chargebacks raised by a payer through their bank are handled under NPCI's dispute-resolution process. Amounts under dispute may be held or debited from the merchant's settlement until the dispute is resolved.
- Failed UPI payouts are credited back to the payout balance.

## Flight Booking

- Cancellation, rescheduling and refund of flight tickets are governed by the fare rules of the airline for the ticket purchased. Some fares are non-refundable.
- Cancellation requests must be raised through the Platform before the airline's cut-off time. Airline cancellation charges, and PC Digital's or the supplier's service fee where applicable, will be deducted from the refund.
- Convenience fees and service charges paid at the time of booking are non-refundable.
- Refunds are credited to the Wallet after the amount is received from the airline or supplier, which typically takes 7 to 21 working days.
- For flights cancelled or rescheduled by the airline, refunds are subject to the airline's policy.
- No refund is due for a "no-show".

## Hotel Booking

- Cancellation and refund of hotel bookings are governed by the cancellation policy of the hotel and rate plan selected, which is shown at the time of booking. Non-refundable bookings cannot be cancelled for a refund.
- Where cancellation is permitted, hotel cancellation charges and applicable service fees will be deducted.
- Refunds are credited to the Wallet after confirmation from the hotel or supplier, typically within 7 to 21 working days.
- No refund is due for a no-show, early check-out or unused nights unless the hotel agrees.

## IRCTC Rail Ticket Booking

- Cancellation of rail e-tickets and filing of Ticket Deposit Receipts (TDR) are governed by the refund rules of Indian Railways and IRCTC.
- E-tickets must be cancelled online through the Platform before chart preparation or within the time permitted by railway rules. Refund amounts are calculated by IRCTC after deducting the applicable cancellation charges.
- IRCTC convenience fees, payment-gateway charges and agent service charges are non-refundable.
- Refunds are credited to the Wallet after they are received from IRCTC. TDR refunds are decided by Indian Railways and may take considerably longer.
- If the booking fails but the amount is debited, it is reversed to the Wallet after reconciliation with IRCTC, typically within 3 to 7 working days.

## Wallet Load Requests

- Wallet credit is given only after funds are received and matched in PC Digital's bank account.
- A wrong, duplicate or excess deposit will be returned to the originating bank account after verification, typically within 7 working days. Bank charges, if any, may be deducted.
- Deposits received from third-party accounts or in cash may be held for verification and returned to the source, in line with our [KYC & AML Policy](kyc-aml-policy.html).

## Onboarding Fees, Devices and Agent IDs

- Any registration, onboarding, activation or agent-ID fee is charged for verification and set-up work and is **non-refundable** once the process has been initiated, including where an application is rejected because of incorrect or insufficient documents provided by the applicant.
- Devices such as biometric scanners purchased through or on the recommendation of PC Digital are covered by the manufacturer's warranty and return policy. PC Digital does not provide refunds for devices.

## Refund of Wallet Balance on Account Closure

On closure of a Partner account, the available Wallet balance will be refunded to the Partner's registered bank account after verification, settlement of pending transactions and disputes, and deduction of any dues, typically within 15 working days of a complete written request. Balances may be withheld where required by law or a competent authority.
