## Who This Policy Applies To

- **Partners** – retailers, agents, distributors, merchants and businesses who register with us.
- **Customers** – individuals who avail a service through a Partner or directly on the Platform.
- **Visitors** – anyone who browses our website or contacts us.

## Information We Collect

### Information you give us

- **Identity and contact details:** name, date of birth, gender, photograph, mobile number, email address, residential and business address.
- **KYC documents:** PAN, Aadhaar (collected only through means permitted by law, with the Aadhaar number masked wherever required), voter ID, driving licence, passport, shop and establishment or business registration documents, GST certificate.
- **Financial details:** bank account number, IFSC, cancelled cheque or bank statement, UPI ID, and transaction details.
- **Business details:** shop name, nature of business, outlet photographs and location.
- **Communications:** the content of your messages, calls, emails and support requests.

### Information collected when a service is used

- **Transaction data:** the service used, amount, date and time, operator or biller, beneficiary details, reference numbers and status.
- **Customer data for a transaction:** mobile number, consumer number, bank name, beneficiary account details, passenger or guest names, and other details needed to complete the service.
- **Aadhaar authentication data:** for AEPS and e-KYC, the Aadhaar number or Virtual ID and biometric or OTP data are captured through registered devices and transmitted in encrypted form to the authorised entity for authentication. **PC Digital does not store biometric data.**

### Information collected automatically

- **Device and log data:** IP address, device type and identifiers, operating system, browser type, app version, access times and pages viewed.
- **Location data:** approximate or precise location of the device, where you permit it, used for fraud prevention and regulatory requirements for agent outlets.
- **Cookies and similar technologies:** see the "Cookies" section below.

### Information from other sources

We may receive information about you from our partner banks and Service Providers, identity-verification and fraud-prevention agencies, public databases, and the distributor who introduced you to us.

## How We Use Your Information

We use personal data for the following purposes:

- to register you, verify your identity and complete KYC and due diligence;
- to provide, process and confirm the transactions and services you request;
- to maintain your Wallet, settle amounts and pay commissions;
- to communicate with you about transactions, service updates, security alerts and support requests;
- to detect, prevent and investigate fraud, money laundering, unauthorised access and other unlawful activity;
- to comply with legal and regulatory obligations, including record-keeping and reporting requirements, and requirements of our partner banks and payment system operators;
- to resolve complaints and disputes, and to enforce our Terms & Conditions;
- to analyse and improve the Platform, our services and user experience; and
- with your consent, to inform you about new products, offers and features. You may opt out of promotional messages at any time.

## Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data on the basis of your consent, or for certain legitimate uses permitted by law, such as performing a service you have asked for, complying with a legal obligation, responding to a court or regulatory order, and safeguarding against fraud. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it as described under "Your Rights".

## How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only as needed, with:

- **Partner banks and payment system participants** – to process AEPS, DMT, UPI, payout and settlement transactions.
- **Operators, billers and BBPS operating units** – to complete recharges and bill payments.
- **Travel suppliers** – airlines, hotels, travel aggregators and authorised rail ticketing service providers, to complete bookings.
- **Verification and technology service providers** – who help us with KYC verification, messaging, cloud hosting, analytics, customer support and security, under confidentiality obligations.
- **Distributors and master distributors** – limited information about Partners in their network, for business operations and support.
- **Regulators, courts and law-enforcement agencies** – where required by law, regulation, legal process or a lawful request.
- **Professional advisers and auditors** – under duties of confidentiality.
- **Successors** – in connection with a merger, acquisition, restructuring or sale of business, subject to this Policy.

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. ## Your Rights

Subject to Applicable Law, you have the right to:

- **access** a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it is processed;
- **correct or update** personal data that is inaccurate or incomplete;
- **erase** personal data that is no longer necessary, unless we are required by law to retain it;
- **withdraw consent** to processing that is based on consent. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out, and may mean we can no longer provide some or all services;
